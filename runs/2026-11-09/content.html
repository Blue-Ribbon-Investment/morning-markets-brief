Economists point to weak domestic demand, especially property, as the reason consumer prices are not picking up. CNBC

Geopolitics

Major geopolitical actions

US–Iran and the Strait of Hormuz

President Trump said on Sunday he is "highly unlikely" to order fresh strikes on Iran as long as talks on its nuclear program and reopening the Strait of Hormuz make progress, while leaving open the option of strikes — including on bridges and power plants. Central Command's "Task Force Iron Gate" naval blockade is "in full force and effect", and both countries reported that shipping had been hit in or near the strait. An end to the war and a reopened strait would likely ease oil prices; markets took a first cue this morning (Brent softer). CNBC · ET

US midterms and their market consequences

The Democrats' "Blue Sweep" of Congress is being read by fund managers as raising the odds of tariff rollback, tighter AI regulation and pressure to end the Iran war — the combination that triggered last week's tech sell-off and this week's rotation into under-owned Indian and Chinese stocks. Business Standard · Mint

US–China tech

Nvidia's board investigation of CEO Jensen Huang sits in the middle of the US–China chip dispute: Megaspeed has been accused of helping restricted Chinese customers obtain Nvidia AI chips. CNBC

Europe

France's protests against living costs entered a fourth straight weekend, with the Interior Ministry counting 146,000 demonstrators and clashes. CNBC

Possible market links (read after forming your own view)

  • Iran/Hormuz de-escalation → softer crude → lower import bill, easier Indian inflation, rupee strength (Brent's ~13% weekly fall is the current tailwind for India).
  • Global AI-capex rout after US midterms → funds rotating into under-owned India and China; last week's ₹46,000-crore FPI inflow into India is the proof so far. Watch whether it sustains.
  • Fed hike odds at ~79% for 9 December → firmer long end of global yield curve; a hawkish tilt is a headwind for emerging-market flows, including India's rally.
  • China's PPI/CPI split (hot wholesale, weak consumer) → weak domestic demand; watch copper and other metals for a confirmation signal.
  • Tariff-rollback rhetoric from the new Congress → risk-off for exporters; an upside for global trade sentiment.
